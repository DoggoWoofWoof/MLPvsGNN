## Why exact rather than approximate

An ANN index is faster but introduces a recall knob, and a candidate pool whose
recall depends on that knob would confound candidate supply with ranking
quality -- the one separation this phase is required to keep. At these sizes
exact scoring is cheap enough that the knob is not worth introducing.

## Declared ceiling and abort criteria

- hard ceiling: 4.0 GPU hours for all five datasets
- abort if measured throughput falls below the low band by more than 2x
- abort if any dataset's recall@K curve is flat in K, which would mean the
  scoring is wrong rather than the corpus hard
- SPLADE retrieval is not priced here: it is sparse, runs on CPU against an
  inverted index, and its cost model is not the one above. It needs its own
  record before it is spent.

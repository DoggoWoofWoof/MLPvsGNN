# Program, 21 October 2026: what is final below the models, and where the points are

This program answers the user's questions of 21 October with filed numbers, and fixes what changes below the models
before the work moves to ranking. The questions:

> have we verified we have the best links and pools build or we are missing something, and universal rules, does it
> help all the datasets and can the models learn and adopt from it, where is the failure how much can we fix and
> expand, i dont want to keep wasting time we need to start finalizing things and then see where is the lever to
> improve in the models

and, about U1c: "how arent we able to adopt ... the links arent bad as well".

Each stage below is declared in its own file and committed before its numbers. Every change is universal: the same
rule on all six datasets, graded on all six, zero-shot included. The encoder stays frozen. Numbers are development
numbers (s1eval unless marked).

## 1. The answers, with the numbers

### 1.1 Are the links the best we can build?

**What is shown.** The links carry what the graph gives the models. Masking the structural family costs R@5 0.17 on
hotpotqa and 2wiki, 0.19 to 0.22 on webqsp and 0.77 on metaqa, for both models
([P2A_LINK_FAMILIES.md](P2A_LINK_FAMILIES.md), section 12). The ner and knn families add pool reach only on musique
(section 11).

**What is not shown.** The LLM-free rule (U1d) was chosen to reproduce the corpus hyperlinks overall (directed F1,
[U1D_PRECISE_LINKS.md](U1D_PRECISE_LINKS.md)). It was never checked against the links the questions follow.
- On B1's small corpora the rule is good: P 0.76 / R 0.81 on 2wiki and P 0.66 / R 0.79 on hotpotqa
  ([U1A_B1_WITHOUT_HYPERLINKS.md](U1A_B1_WITHOUT_HYPERLINKS.md)).
- On the full corpora it is not. U1d's chosen V2 finds 0.675 of hotpotqa's hyperlinks and 0.566 of 2wiki's. Of its own
  links, 0.68 (hotpotqa) and 0.77 (2wiki) are not hyperlinks.

### 1.2 Why U1c and U1f were not adopted: the pools lost the golds

U1c retrained the 10 October bases (zrc, zsp) on the LLM-free graph with step 4h's pools. U1f repeated it on the
damped graph. Both were NOT_ADOPTED. They found no GAIN, and zsp lost webqsp zero-shot. Both arms lost hotpotqa and
2wiki ([U1C_RETRAIN_ON_U.md](U1C_RETRAIN_ON_U.md), [U1F_RETRAIN_ON_GU1024.md](U1F_RETRAIN_ON_GU1024.md)).

**The split.** R@5 is the pool's gold recall times the share of in-pool golds ranked in the top five.
- The **reach** part is the recall change at the base's share.
- The **ranking** part is the share change at the new recall.
- The recalls are step 4h's filed coverage (`outputs/step4h/coverage_<D>.json`, s1eval): step 4e's pools, which the
  bases read, against GU's W1, which U1c read.
- The R@5 values are the four filed screens (`outputs/screen/scr-u1c-*.json`).
- The split assumes the lost golds were ranked like the rest. The exact split needs the per-question arrays, which are
  on the host.

U1c's change in R@5 (in R@5 points, the four arms' range):

| dataset | change | reach (golds the pool lost) | ranking (golds still in the pool) | pool recall, base -> GU |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| hotpotqa | −4.0 to −6.3 | −3.6 to −3.8 | −0.4 to −2.5 | 0.986 -> 0.944 |
| 2wiki | −1.1 to −2.0 | −2.3 | **+0.4 to +1.2** | 0.979 -> 0.953 |
| metaqa | +0.4 to +0.7 | −0.7 | +1.1 to +1.4 | 0.988 -> 0.979 |
| webqsp (zero-shot) | −5.6 (zsp, L-musique) | −0.9 | −4.8 | 0.966 -> 0.940 |
| webqsp, the other three arms | −0.8 to +1.0 | −0.6 to −0.9 | +0.1 to +1.6 | |

What this says:
- **The models do learn from the LLM-free links.** On 2wiki and metaqa they rank the golds they are given better
  than on the old graph.
- **The loss is reach.** On the full corpora the rule does not link the bridge passages that a hyperlink led to, so
  they never enter the pool. Even the base pool (the first-stage rows and their link neighbours, before KIND_T or any
  walk) holds 4.1 points fewer of hotpotqa's golds on GU (0.980 -> 0.939) and 2.2 fewer of 2wiki's (0.964 -> 0.942).
- **The one ranking failure is the GNN on webqsp.** zsp propagates over 311 million mention links beside 8.3 million
  KB triples.
- **U1c never tested today's system** on that graph: today's pools (KIND_T), the KT input, the lean pair and the free
  GNN all came after it.

### 1.4 Is everything universal?

**Everything since 9 October is one rule for all six:** the link rule, KIND_T's order, both models and the KT input.

**One thing is not: the base every pool starts from.** It is M3B's per-dataset contract of 13 September
(`configs/m3b_controlled_comparison.yaml`, `candidate_contract_frozen_2026_09_13`, read by `scripts/m3b_compile.py`'s
`prepare`). The contract fixes:
- the first-stage list and its size: RRF top 50 (metaqa, squad, hotpotqa, 2wiki), RRF top 200 (musique), dense top
  200 (webqsp);
- the link families the base expands over: structural (metaqa, hotpotqa, 2wiki), all three (musique, webqsp), none
  (squad).

That base sets each dataset's pool size, |I_q|, which KIND_T fills to: 50 (squad) to 2,120 (webqsp) rows. P1g's and
P1g2's fill fractions are multiples of the same sizes.
- A new dataset has no rule for its base.
- webqsp's zero-shot reads use webqsp's own contract.

This is the last universality debt below the models.

### 1.5 Where the failure is, and how much is there

The lean pair's card fits on today's pools (`base R@5` in [P1GB_POOLS_REFIT.md](P1GB_POOLS_REFIT.md), section 7)
against the pools' gold recall (section 1.3).
- metaqa's and webqsp's questions can have more than five golds, so R@5 has a cap below 1. The cap is the mean over
  s1eval's questions of min(5, |golds|) / |golds|, from the package's queries.

| dataset | golds in the pool | R@5, in domain (zero-shot) | in-pool golds in the top five |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| 2wiki | 0.984 | 0.935 | 0.95 |
| squad | 0.980 | 0.912 | 0.93 |
| hotpotqa | 0.976 | 0.907 (0.851) | 0.93 (0.87) |
| musique | 0.963 | 0.575 (0.512) | **0.60 (0.53)** |
| metaqa | 0.989 | 0.802 | 0.97 of its cap, 0.830 (0.281 of questions have more than five golds) |
| webqsp | 0.919 | zero-shot only: 0.268 to 0.363 | **0.30 to 0.41 of its cap, 0.887** |

**The lever is ranking, on three fronts:**
- **musique.** About 0.39 of its golds sit in the pool and outside the top five. On B1 the GNN is at 66.3 against
  HippoRAG 2's 74.7.
- **Zero-shot transfer.** webqsp is at 0.30 to 0.41 of its cap. Held out, hotpotqa is 0.055 under its in-domain read.
- **Bridges.** D2 (8 October) found the inputs of that time used up: trees on them and the score gained at most 0.006.
  D1 found the missed golds linked to found ones. What the models lack is how a row relates to the question through
  another strong row. R2 ([R2_BRIDGE_EM.md](R2_BRIDGE_EM.md)) tests that in both models. G6
  ([G6_FREE_GNN.md](G6_FREE_GNN.md)) tests a GNN that passes hidden states.

**Below the models, what is left:**
- the LLM-free graph's reach: up to 3.8 points of R@5 on hotpotqa and 2.3 on 2wiki (section 1.2);
- the universal base (section 1.4): its price is not measured.

### 1.6 Where the three claims stand

- **Claim 1 (the MLP keeps at least 90% of the GNN's gain).**
  - Full corpora ([P3C_CLAIM1_INTERVALS.md](P3C_CLAIM1_INTERVALS.md)): it holds on metaqa, hotpotqa and 2wiki, and
    zero-shot on all six. It is not shown on squad (the GNN's gain is 0.0045) or musique (0.933 [0.881, 0.983]).
  - B1 ([B1E_B1_INTERVALS.md](B1E_B1_INTERVALS.md)): it holds on 2wiki (0.970 [0.953, 0.987]) and hotpotqa (0.992
    [0.967, 1.020]). It is not shown on musique (0.983 [0.890, 1.079]).
- **Claim 2 (an LLM-free GNN at SOTA), B1 against HippoRAG 2:**
  - With the hyperlinks: above on 2wiki (97.2 against 90.4) and hotpotqa (98.2 against 96.3), below on musique (66.3
    against 74.7).
  - With the LLM-free rule and the same fits: 95.9 on 2wiki (above) and 95.0 on hotpotqa (below).
  - The B1 GNN is zsp, the MLP plus one propagation step. G6's free GNN has no B1 read yet.
- **Claim 3 (the MLP is 2-3x faster, cold).** C12: the MLP is 2.38 to 3.99 times faster than the hidden-state GNN
  (gnn0), and 0.96 to 1.18 times zsp's speed. zsp is the MLP plus one step, so the claim needs a GNN of its own (G6).

2. **G6 and R2** (queued). The free GNN, and bridge transforms trained with EM in both models.
3. **P1h** (queued): the same coverage in fewer rows.
4. **U2: the LLM-free graph, bounded.** One file per phase.
   - **U2a (diagnostic, next file).** For every gold the LLM-free graph's base loses on hotpotqa and 2wiki, it records
     which step of the rule drops the hyperlink that reached it, and whether ner, knn or a question-guided hop from its
     parent still reaches it. No training, and nothing is chosen.
   - **U2b.** At most one universal rule change, if U2a points to one.
   - **U2c.** One rebuild of today's pools, KT input and lean pair on the LLM-free graph. The rule that decides the
     headline graph is fixed in its file before any number.
   - If U2c cannot hold 2wiki and hotpotqa within its rule, the hyperlinks stay, named, and the link work closes.
5. **P1i: one base for all six (pools only).** One contract for every dataset, measured against today's coverage at
   the same row budget before anything trains. It enters the final rebuild only if its own rule passes.
6. **After that, the models only.** Each idea gets one screen and is graded on all six, zero-shot included.

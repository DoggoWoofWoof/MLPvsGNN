## 6. The selected minimal repair, if any

**Condition B was not measurable from what Stage 0 produced, and the gate said so before the results existed.** The probe ranks four whole models; B asks about a single primitive. A stop resting on an unmeasured condition is not a stop, so the measurement was made afterwards on the two failure cells and the same gate was re-applied unchanged.

Each figure is the share of the queries S4 gets wrong at rank 1, with a relevant candidate in the pool, on which that primitive ALONE ranks a relevant candidate above the item S4 put first. The bar is a majority (> 0.50) on both, and both the bar and the admission rule were filed before any of these numbers existed.

| primitive | squad_clean/R1 (n=5,811) | musique_clean/R1 (n=699) | majority on both |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| `semantic_difference` | 0.5949 | 0.7482 | **yes** |
| `semantic_product` | 0.3543 | 0.7067 | no |
| `mean_abs_diff` | 0.3442 | 0.7253 | no |
| `cosine_qd` | 0.2857 | 0.7067 | no |
| `dot_qd_pct` | 0.2853 | 0.7067 | no |

Measured and not admitted, because B is about a primitive S4 is MISSING: `normalized_state_dot`. It is the negative control -- S4's own cosine between its projected, GELU-warped states -- and it behaves like one: 0.4858 on squad_clean/R1, 0.6094 on musique_clean/R1. S4's own geometry does not rescue S4.

**The selected minimal repair is `semantic_difference`** -- S3's learned weighted L1 over all 1,536 raw coordinates, ranked as a distance because that is its form. It is one of the two learned S3 diagonals, and it is the only primitive of the five that clears the bar on both blockers.

1 of 5 primitives S4 is missing reach a majority on both failure cells. A pass by one of 5 is a selection over 5 and is reported as such. Three things this does NOT say, each of which would be a different claim:

*   It does not say the raw geometry is enough. `cosine_qd`, `dot_qd_pct`, `mean_abs_diff` are the parameter-free primitives and every one of them fails the bar on SQuAD, the best of the three reaching 0.3442. Section 7 asked whether cheap raw geometry alone suffices before anything expensive is added, and on this evidence it does not.
*   It does not say the other learned diagonal is implicated. `semantic_product` reaches 0.3543 on SQuAD and fails.
*   It does not say a sole ranker's behaviour transfers to an added column. B asks what a primitive does ALONE, which is a harder test than being one input among many, and also a different one. Whether adding it to S4 recovers the blocker is what Stage 1 would measure, and nothing here has measured it.

One artefact worth stating so it is not read as three findings: on MuSiQue, `cosine_qd`, `dot_qd_pct` and `semantic_product` reorder exactly the same number of queries (494). On SQuAD they separate, so they are not one ranker in general; but this probe records counts and not per-query agreement, so whether MuSiQue's three coincide query-for-query is not something these artifacts can answer.

**S4 is not small.** It is 57 times the incumbent's total parameter count. The semantic branch is 196,608 parameters against S3's 3,072, and the width of that branch costs again in the scorer: at 32 parameters per semantic column, derived from S2 and S3 and confirmed against S4, S4's 258 columns cost 8,160 scorer parameters beyond S2's three. Both halves are carried here because section 14 requires it and because a phase arguing for a cheap repair should be the last to understate what it is repairing.

What the repair itself would add, if Stage 1 ran it, in the ladder's own declared terms: at most 2 * 1536 for the S3-diagonal arm -- and, since only one of the two diagonals is implicated, 1,536 plus one scorer input weight is the minimum. For the raw-scalar arm, at most one scorer input weight per added scalar.

# Program, 15 October 2026: a GNN that uses message passing, musique's hops, KB transfer, final pools

This program answers the user's questions of 15 October with filed numbers, and orders the stages that follow. Each
stage is declared in its own file and committed before its numbers. Every change is universal: the same rule on all
six datasets, graded on all six, zero-shot included. The encoder stays frozen; no LLM or transformer enters a model.

## 1. Answers, with the numbers

### 1.1 Why is the GNN not better than the MLP?

**Because our GNN passes scores, not representations.** The lean GNN, zspk, is the lean MLP's network plus one
propagation step. That step gives each row the mean, the soft maximum and the count of its neighbours' z-scored scores
per link family, and a 385-weight head turns those ten numbers into a correction. So the only thing that travels along
an edge is one number per row, the score the MLP already computed.

The result is that the two models are almost the same model. On the 36 filed reads of P3c (docs/P3C_CLAIM1_INTERVALS.md,
`outputs/p3c/claim1.json`), zspk and zrckp are within 0.008 R@5 of each other on every in-domain read:

| dataset (J5, in-domain) | zrckp (MLP) | zspk (GNN) | GNN − MLP |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| metaqa | 0.7989 | 0.8012 | +0.0023 |
| squad | 0.9112 | 0.9099 | −0.0013 |
| musique | 0.5728 | 0.5800 | +0.0072 |
| hotpotqa | 0.9065 | 0.9044 | −0.0021 |
| 2wiki | 0.9304 | 0.9351 | +0.0047 |

**Deeper score passing did not help either.** zg1 (SCREENS.md, the thirty-first round) stacked four NBFNet-style layers
with 32-wide states, 17,025 parameters, but started them from the same score summaries. Result: NO_GAIN. 2wiki moved
+0.006 and +0.007, under the 0.0075 floor; webqsp zero-shot lost 0.016; musique did not move.

**A GNN that passes hidden states does better where links matter.** gnn0, the universal GNN (three relation-aware
layers over 129 input columns and the 1,536-wide embeddings, 420,932 parameters, trained on all six), reaches 0.891 R@5
on 2wiki against zsp's 0.873 (P3a), with fewer inputs than zsp. It is below zsp on musique (0.538 against 0.566) and
hotpotqa (0.899 against 0.905). So neither form is the answer yet: the residual over scores is too thin, and the
standalone GNN lacks the lean models' inputs.

**Does the GNN have to be built on the MLP?** Every GNN starts with a per-node encoder: a small network that turns each
node's inputs into a vector before any message is passed. Ours shares that encoder with the MLP on purpose: same
inputs, same encoder, so the MLP-versus-GNN difference is exactly what message passing adds (Claim 1's δ_MP). What is
wrong today is not the shared encoder. It is that the GNN's message passing sees only the encoder's final score.
**Stage G2** passes the encoder's 128-wide hidden state instead, through three question-gated layers per link family,
and trains the whole network together. The GNN keeps the MLP's encoder, so the MLP stays exactly the GNN without
message passing, and stays faster.

### 1.2 Where we stand against SOTA GNNs

**We do not know the gap to trained SOTA GNNs yet.** P3a's bar is LLM-free rankers rebuilt on our data (PPR, HippoRAG
2's restart walk and others), not trained GNNs. B2, running GFM-RAG's and G-reasoner's released GNNs on our encoder and
graphs (docs/PAPER_CLAIMS_2026_10_09.md), was never run. On HippoRAG 2's own benchmark (B1b) zsp is above HippoRAG 2
on 2wiki (97.2 against 90.4) and hotpotqa (98.2 against 96.3) and below it on musique (66.3 against 74.7). **Stage B2**
measures the trained-GNN gap.

### 1.3 musique: what goes wrong, by hop

From docs/MUSIQUE_DIAGNOSIS.md (seed 0, on the pools of that time):

| hops | share of questions | all golds in the pool | R@5 (`rrf` / GNN) | δ_MP on R@5 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- | --- |
| 2 | 52% | 0.941 | 0.562 / 0.643 | +0.046 |
| 3 | 31% | 0.746 | 0.437 / 0.445 | −0.018 |
| 4 | 17% | 0.481 | 0.269 / 0.286 | −0.004 |

Four problems, each with its evidence:
1. **The pool misses the far hops.** A four-hop question has all its golds in the pool 48% of the time.
2. **Message passing helps only two-hop questions.** With two layers, a three- or four-hop chain cannot be followed.
3. **Training sees the wrong mix.** musique's training questions are 72% two-hop, against 52% in dev. Three- and
   four-hop questions are 28% of training and 48% of dev.
4. **Training rewards a shortcut dev does not have.** Training questions share single-hop components (the same
   sub-question appears in many training questions), and dev shares none of them with training (0 of 2,768). A fit can
   memorize component passages; that part, 0.18 to 0.19 of R@5, does not carry to dev.

D4 (13 October) found that the words on a link do not pick the neighbour a musique question follows. The first stage
starts 13.3 points below NV-Embed-v2 on B1. **Stage M1** repeats the hop table on KIND_T's pools with the lean pair,
since these numbers predate both.

**M1's answer (15 October, docs/M1_MUSIQUE_HOPS.md section 6).** On today's pools and models, a musique question's
first paragraph is found (0.70 to 0.84 in the top five). The chain breaks at the bridge: the next paragraph is
usually one or two links from the found one (0.56 of musique's missed golds; 0.50 on hotpotqa, 0.69 on 2wiki).
zspk lifts those no more often than the MLP: a net 111 of 30,654 golds on 2wiki, −31 on hotpotqa, +46 on musique.
The one propagation step does not use the link that is there, and that is the case for G2 and E1. The 4-hop chains
also run out of pool (0.22 of the last sub-question's golds are out), and zero-shot both models push the answer of a
3- or 4-hop chain below rrf.

### 1.4 webqsp: what helps it, and how other datasets can use it

webqsp never trains in the lean pair, so every webqsp number is zero-shot. What moved it:

| change | effect on webqsp | general rule it rests on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| steps 4 and 4b: a restart walk over all three link families | pool all-gold 0.688 to 0.782 | walk every family |
| KIND_T's entity route | the pool the lean pair reads | trust links the first stage corroborates |
| P1c3p's KT input (walk positions) | up on all six fits | relation-free structure transfers |
| metaqa in training | 0.35 against 0.11 without it | a KB in training teaches KB structure |
| S6 granularity transforms (design look) | +6.43 over the untyped walk | match relation text at several granularities |
| training on webqsp (gnn0, universal pair) | 0.629 against 0.29 to 0.37 | (not zero-shot) |

The lesson that carries is the third and fourth rows: **inputs that describe structure without naming a relation
transfer, and a KB in training is what teaches KB structure.** With metaqa held out (L-metaqa), webqsp falls to 0.114
(MLP) and 0.116 (GNN), from about 0.35 when metaqa trains.

### 1.5 Wikipedia to metaqa: the generalization gap

With metaqa held out, the models read metaqa at **0.249 (MLP) and 0.213 (GNN)**, against 0.80 in-domain: a gap of
0.55 to 0.59 R@5. The passage graphs teach "follow a hyperlink or a title mention to a passage". A KB question is
answered by a chain of typed relations ("films starring the actor who starred in X"), and nothing in a passage graph
teaches which relation name matches which words of the question. The two general levers we have:
- **relation text as edge text on every graph:** a KB edge carries its relation's name, embedded with the frozen
  encoder; a passage edge carries its family's name. The GNN's gate then matches the question against the edge's
  text, which can transfer to an unseen KB because matching text is what every dataset trains. This is stage G2r,
  after G2;
- **granularity transforms in training** (stage T1): S6 showed matching a relation at several granularities carries to
  webqsp (+6.43, design look). It was never wired into the lean models.

### 1.6 Why the transforms and the offsets did not work

- **The offsets** (P5b, P5b2, P5b3) learned a vector g(question, seed, graph) to jump from a seed to the answer without
  walking. They memorized: their gains came on golds seen in training. P5b2 held them out: 2wiki's and metaqa's gains
  held on new golds, but musique sat near chance. P5b3's back-off gate gained on four datasets and still lost on musique
  (−0.005 to −0.011). Under the all-six rule, learned offsets are closed.
- **The transforms did not fail in the models: they were never tried there.** S6 tested them in a standalone chain
  scorer as design looks (+6.43 on webqsp zero-shot against the untyped walk; +10.7 against the same scorer without
  transforms; −4.8 on metaqa in-domain). Stage T1 wires them into training as an augmentation of both models.
- **EM was used only in side tracks.** The S6 chain scorer and the type-factorized track trained with EM over a latent
  chain (which path reaches the gold). The lean models train with a listwise loss on gold labels only: no latent path.
  **Stage E1** adds the path as a latent variable to G2: the E-step infers which edges carried each gold, the M-step
  trains the gates on those paths. It needs G2's gates first.

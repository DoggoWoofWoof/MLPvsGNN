### The shape of the tail

Dense truncates at 32,768 tokens -- the model
default, because `canonical_encode.py` passes `max_seq=None`. Not 256. That
matters, because `SentenceTransformer.encode` sorts by length before batching,
so the longest documents are batched *with each other*.

| representation | mean | p50 | p99 | max | rows over cap | peak batch slots | peak activations (GiB) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `name_only` | 22.5 | 17 | 107 | 2,445 | 0 | 78,240 | 6.3 |
| `name_plus_facts` | 56.8 | 25 | 530 | 619,684 | 31 | 1,048,576 | 84.0 |

### Hazards

> name_plus_facts: the dense peak batch is 1,048,576 padded token-slots, about 84.0 GiB of activations against the 24.0 GiB assumed card. SentenceTransformer sorts by length, so the longest documents batch with each other and this fires on the FIRST batch, deterministically, not at random late in the run. Encoding this representation needs an explicit --max-seq or a smaller batch, and both depart from the frozen contract, so both are review decisions.

## Which representation

The two options are not two prices for the same thing. name_plus_facts folds incident triples into the node's own text, which moves relational information into the SEMANTIC channel. QLS-U would then receive graph structure through its embeddings whether or not a structural feature is ever declared, and delta_MP would stop isolating what it is meant to isolate -- the GNN's advantage would already be inside the baseline's inputs. That is a scientific objection rather than a cost one, and it points at name_only, but the call belongs to review.

## The rate, assumed

This box is CPU-only, so a local benchmark of a 1.5B encoder would not predict GPU throughput, and no shard status JSON in the package records a timing -- they carry rows, sha, dtype and dim only. The calibration shard replaces the assumption with a measurement before most of the cost is spent.

Declared: dense 5,000-15,000 padded tokens/GPU-second,
SPLADE 70,000-200,000, at
$1.1/GPU-hour on a 24.0 GiB card.
Status **DECLARED_NOT_MEASURED**. Derivation: a forward pass costs about 2*params FLOPs per token; at an effective 15-45 TFLOP/s on an A10G-class card that is 5k-15k tokens/s for the 1.5B dense encoder and 70k-200k for the 110M splade encoder.

Hard ceiling **16.2 dense GPU hours** (twice the slow end of the more expensive representation).
Exceeding the ceiling aborts the job; it does not trigger a raise.

### Calibration before commitment

1. encode shard 0 only, both modalities, under the frozen contract
2. measure real padded-token throughput and real storage per shard
3. re-derive the full estimate from the measurement
4. file the re-derived estimate
5. only then release the remaining shards

### Abort criteria

- measured throughput below the low end of the declared range
- re-derived total above the hard ceiling
- any shard failing the finite/shape check canonical_encode.py already raises
- dimension not 1536, or stored dtype not float16
- row count of a finished shard not equal to its id list length
- an out-of-memory on the length-sorted peak batch

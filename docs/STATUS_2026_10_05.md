## Main changes (systems)

- **Remote execution on the shared lab GPU host** (`tools/rx`).
  - Projects carry priorities, and the GPU is hidden from CPU jobs.
  - The host CPU is equivalent to the laptop within tolerance.
  - The host GPU is task-equivalent and reproducible in training. It is used only for stages declared host-native.
- **A feeder running on the host** (`outputs/host_ops/hostfeed.py`) sends a queue of declared items in file order, with
  dependencies. It keeps the queue moving while the laptop is off.
  - An auto-cleanup loop and an hourly sync run beside it.
  - A host recovery guide and restore script cover restarting on another host.
- **A retry wrapper** (`outputs/host_ops/retry_cmd.py`) reruns a job that failed on a transient Windows file race.
  - Since 5 Oct it also reruns GPU jobs that hit a CUDA fault, with `--cuda`.
  - The reason: two short GPU jobs died on "illegal memory access" on 5 Oct, and in the feeder one failed run drops
    every grade that reads it.
- **Fast feature compile** (`fast_features.py`, f4fc3c2): the CRAG feature contract bit for bit, 4–16× faster per
  query. Wiring it into a phase needs its own declared stage.
- **Exact serving forms for the lean MLP**: fused kernels whose top-5 is bit-identical to the training form.
- **Exploratory code under the gitignored `outputs/` tree** is tracked from c11ab5c on.

## Results (development-only)

### Declared phases

- **M2B:** `SELECTED_S3` (3,585 parameters).
- **M2C and M2D:** S4 repair closed on both routes (`STOP_S4_DEVELOPMENT_CONFIRMED`). S3 stays.
- **M3B:** the message-passing delta in R@5 is read on musique, hotpotqa and 2wiki, and not read on metaqa or webqsp.
  Message passing lifts full_coverage@5, not hit@1.
- **Universal-v2:**
  - UMLP-v2.1 and v2.2A returned `STAGE_A_FAIL`, and the pilot returned `PILOT_FAILED`.
  - The stage 2 joint six-dataset GNN ran, with hit@1 gains on metaqa and webqsp.
  - Evidence flow is structurally zero on the passage graphs.
- **MP-Approx L0 to L15** (approximations of the GNN's effect without message passing):
  - L15's best arm, FZ-TW-1x-b1d, reaches 0.929 and is BELOW the GNN on R@5 and full_coverage@5.
  - On 2wiki, anchor relation types about double rho (0.27 to about 0.70).
  - Zero-shot transfer across graphs is poor.
- **Deploy CK on 2wiki:**
  - The compiled kernel ck_qi keeps 0.093 of the GNN's R@5 gain.
  - ck_full, a compressed, query-conditioned one-hop message-passing form, keeps 0.895 of the GNN's R@5 gain at 0.656
    of the GNN's end-to-end p50 latency. Both are measured in their training forms.

### The MLP and GNN development tracks

- **Lean MLP:**
  - In-domain on 2wiki, the AW pick reaches R@5 0.9964 relative to the GNN (rho 0.865).
  - Zero-shot is the weak side: AW adds nothing, and hotpot falls 9.3 points.
  - The speed advantage depends on the regime. At one thread the pick runs at 0.35–0.58× the GNN's fast form. In the
    features-only (GPU or large-batch) limit it runs at 0.88–0.93×, which is not faster. The fused kernel's warm p5A
    path takes 0.138× of the GNN's total.
- **QD-GNN / MP-unified:**
  - On 2wiki, QD over the twin is about level with the six-dataset GNN in-domain.
  - Edge-family dropout is the first one-graph message-passing arm with no unseen graph below the twin on R@5.
  - The joint four-graph W4 arm sits +0.51 (2wiki) and +0.57 (hotpot) over the twin.
  - Unseen KB relations remain the open problem.
- **Transfer without labels (S1 to S6, metaqa as the training KB, webqsp as the zero-shot KB):**
  - The zero-shot harm on hotpot traces to SEMB, and every label-free shift rule tried fails.
  - Held-out likelihood is blind to label switching and to granularity level. Only text (end text, names) or
    availability attaches a cluster to a relation.
  - **Part 3:** the EM chain scorer with granularity transforms is +6.43 R@5 over the untyped walk on webqsp (selectf
    + fit), zero-shot. The transforms are what transfers (+10.7), at a cost of 4.8 points in-domain.
  - **Part 4:** the GNN scorer with EM-typed attention carries, at +3.5 over the MLP scorer on the same read.
  - **Part 5:** typed passages in training hurt the KB zero-shot read (−3.3), and lb carries (+2.3).
  - **Part 6:** pq carries (+3.9 [+2.8, +5.1] on webqsp selectf + fit, metaqa not BELOW).
  - **Part 8:** more training distributions carry nothing.
  - Parts 7 and 9 to 11 ran or are running on the host, and their grades have not been read yet.
- **S6 part 14, first interim read** (`cs30-static`, 5 Oct 12:22; the MLP and sg2 classes only; the full grade on 7
  Oct is the record). R@5 points, webqsp_sf primary, metaqa guard.
  - **One more EM round at read time** (3 instead of the trained 2) carries for both classes, but the gain is small:
    metaqa +0.17 and +0.32, webqsp_sf level.
  - **Re-estimating the level mix** on the new graph's unlabeled questions (prior-shift EM) is BELOW on webqsp_sf (−5.07
    and −3.05). The damped version is level everywhere. Neither carries.
  - **sg2's static neighbour summaries** hurt the zero-shot KB read beyond one step: K = 1 carries (+4.43), K = 3 and 4
    are BELOW (−3.28, −6.17). Forward-only summaries carry (+2.62). Part 14 chooses K = 1 and forward only.
  - The training arms' intervals are row bootstraps over seed-averaged rows. They leave out seed-to-seed training
    variation.

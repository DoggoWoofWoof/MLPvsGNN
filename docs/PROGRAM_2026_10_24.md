## 2. The three edge families

The same three families are used on every dataset.

1. **EL, entity links.** One entity vocabulary: titles and their forms under W2's gates, U2i's aliases once graded,
   and spaCy's keys where they are built. Each node gets one mention set. Two edge kinds:
   - **IS** (u mentions E, and v owns E): kept at any df, as links are now.
   - **CO** (u and v both mention E): weighted by 1/df, with NER's cap.
   On the KBs, a node's text is its name, so EL comes from name containment, and the question links to nodes through
   the same vocabulary. CVT mediators get no EL edge.
2. **R, relational.** The stored typed edges: the KB triples, each with its relation and direction. On a passage
   graph this means the sibling links (passages of one article).
3. **kNN.** The frozen encoder's neighbours, unchanged.

The three hyperlink-gap steps become parts of EL:
- U2i's aliases are more IS surfaces;
- U2j's link precision orders IS edges;
- U2k's learned link scorer scores IS edges.
Their declared passes and gates stand as filed.

type fits the question.
- A KB relation's type has the encoder's text vector.
- Every other edge has no text vector. It gets a latent type that EM fits on the graph, with no questions and no
  golds. The same EM is run on the KB's relations as a second form.
- A7's k-means pseudo-types are the filed form these latent types are compared with, beside the untyped walk.
  - A7's types steered a scorer, never a pool walk, so U2m is a new form.
  - A7's passage reads were flat, so U2m's prior on passage graphs is weak.
- With every weight equal, the walk is KIND_T's, bit for bit.

**The list-whole cut (U2m b).** The cut keeps or drops a group whole. A group is the nodes reached from one parent by
one edge type (on the KB, one entity's objects of one relation).

## 4. The two models: each built as its own best model

Both models are worked on, and each is built to be as good as it can be. They share the inputs, pools and carves,
not an architecture.

### 4.1 The GNN is its own model, not the MLP plus a step

This is the user's ruling of 20 October:

> for speed do we really have to make the gnn use the mlp as base because i felt we are restricting the gnn else why
> are we so much worse than the other sota models

It stands here unchanged.

**What restricted the GNN.**
- zsp, zspk and zspx are the lean MLP's network plus propagation steps over the neighbours' z-scored scores.
- G2 (zg2) and A10 (zg2x) put hidden-state layers over that score, with the readout bounded to ±0.5 around it. Both
  found no GAIN.
- So the lean GNN stayed within 0.004 R@5 of zrckp on every in-domain read. It was below zrckp on all four zero-shot
  reads, by −0.0109 to −0.0795 (docs/G6_FREE_GNN.md section 1).
- Where the GNN was its own model, it pulled ahead. The universal gnn0 has three relation-aware hidden-state layers
  trained end to end. It beat its MLP twin twin0 by +0.007 to +0.063 R@5 on all six datasets, and twin0 kept only
  0.596 of gnn0's R@5 gain on musique (docs/P3A_SOTA_RANKERS.md).

**The rule for every GNN form from here on.**
- Its own encoder.
- Multi-layer hidden-state message passing: attention, edge-type-aware, with the row's boundary inputs (the KT input,
  the chain features) entering every layer.
- Its own unbounded readout, trained from scratch.
- No MLP score anywhere in its forward pass, and no residual bounded around one.

zsp, zspk and zspx stay filed as the hybrid track (the MLP plus message passing). They are not the GNN line.

**The GNN line.**
- **G6** (docs/G6_FREE_GNN.md, `zg6.py`; committed 5cae567) is the first form: zg6, and zg6t, the same model with no
  edges reaching its layers. G6's encoder, context, boundary and three G2-style layers are kept, as is fd25's
  edge-family dropout.
- G6 has not run. The lab host went offline on 20 October. On Modal, U2h's zg6 fits were cut, and the four zg6w fits
  were lost to workspace 4's spend limit (docs/U2H_FULL_RUN.md section 8).

**Why message passing belongs in the EM over edge types.** This is the user's logic.
- On a passage graph an edge's type is not observed, so it is a latent variable.
- The E-step's posterior over an edge u → v's type can read both ends' hidden states and the question's context. Only
  message passing gives the model those states, so in the GNN the posterior is q(z | h_u, h_v, c), not a fixed
  cluster label.
- The M-step trains one message map W_z and one type vector per latent type inside the fit.
- The typed messages then steer what flows along each edge, as gnn0's relation-aware layers do with observed
  relations.
- On the KB, the observed relation is the prior over z: its text vector lives in the same space as the type vectors.
  So the passage edges and the KB's triples train one relation channel, and webqsp's 7,058 relations meet a channel
  trained on every dataset's edges, not only metaqa's 9.
- The graph-only EM types from U2m are this model's starting point, not a fixed input. Fitting offsets separately and
  feeding them in as a fixed column is P5b's and P5c's failed form.

**Claim 1** is measured against the strongest GNN. If the MLP keeps less than 0.90 of that GNN's gain, that is the
honest result. The MLP's job is then to close the gap without message passing. The 20 October ruling's fairness
holds: each model at its best and fastest exact form, on a common input set (docs/F1_ONE_INPUT_SET.md), never a shared
architecture.

### 4.2 The MLP keeps its rules and gets its own relation channel

The rules stand (docs/SCREENS.md):
- no message passing;
- no input that reads another row's score;
- no per-question normalisation outside the training loss.

**Its relation channel** reads, per row, aggregates of the row's own edges to B by type. On the KB the type is the
relation; on a passage graph it is U2m's latent type. All of it is computed before any row is scored. R2's EM over
the latent path runs inside the MLP's own fit, per row.

**Carried in, each as its own declared form:**
- zrckp (the KT input and its pointwise head, P1c3p);
- the typed relation columns;
- A5c's coupled kind offsets;
- R1's bridge residual columns (zrckpr).

Whether R2's zrckpb stays in the MLP track is still the user's ruling: it reads the 5 first-stage bridges' frozen
vectors (docs/R2_BRIDGE_EM.md).

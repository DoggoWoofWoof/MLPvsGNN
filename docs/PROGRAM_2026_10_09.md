# The program from 9 October 2026: a GNN at SOTA, an MLP close to it, both universal

Written 9 October 2026, from the user's goals of the same day, before any stage below runs. Each stage gets its own
declared file, committed before its numbers. The standing rules hold:
- The encoder stays frozen. No LLM or transformer is trained or called in our system.
- Training changes are general: the same change on all six datasets, graded on all six, zero-shot included.
- One fit per idea first (a screen), then a full run only after a promising screen.
- Latency is cold.

## The goals

1. **GNN to SOTA.** Find what the GNN lacks against the published systems, and add it.
2. **MLP close to the GNN.** No message passing in the MLP, on a harder objective than SGC, SIGN, GLNN, SA-MLP and RTA
   use. Those papers do node classification; ours is set retrieval on unseen questions and unseen graphs.
3. **A semantic space built from the graph.** Vector retrieval in a space where relations act as offsets or
   transformations (parallelogram rule, relation-conditioned attention), so a query needs no graph walk.
4. **One representation for passages and KB facts,** so what is learned on one kind of graph carries to the other.
5. **Better pools and one joint feature selection** for both models: chosen across all six datasets, zero-shot
   included.
6. **A gap ledger across the three families (SOTA, GNN, MLP).** For each dataset and hop slice, every miss is placed in
   one of four buckets:
   - not in the pool;
   - in the pool and reachable from a found gold, but not ranked;
   - beyond the features;
   - top rank only.
7. **Speed, cold.** The MLP's cold end-to-end lead over the GNN is measured, and made real if it is not there yet.
   Target: 6–9× at batch 1.

Papers: a diagnostic short paper (WWW, "how much message passing"), then an analytical and systems paper with the
better models (SIGIR / NeurIPS).

## What we take from the published systems

| system | what it has that our GNN lacks |
| --- | --- |
| GFM-RAG / G-reasoner | query-dependent message passing (NBFNet-style: initialised at the question's entities, relation-conditioned messages), 6 layers rather than our 2, pretraining over many graphs |
| HippoRAG 2 | a personalized PageRank over the whole graph, with every retrieved passage in the restart (step 4e takes this) |
| PullNet / KGFR | iterative pool expansion around linked entities (step 4e takes the linking) |
| KB SOTA (ReaRev, NuTrea) | typed relations; small, query-conditioned models. They are also given the topic entity, an oracle we do not have |

The LLM-built parts (OpenIE triples, LLM filters) are not taken. That we need none is part of the claim.

## The stages, in order

| B1 | **benchmark alignment**: HippoRAG 2's published question sets and corpora (MuSiQue, 2Wiki, HotpotQA), with passage graphs built by our existing method, so our R@5 sits beside HippoRAG 2, GFM-RAG, G-reasoner, HGRAG and PropRAG on their own numbers | laptop, then host | the user's OK to download the files |

| L1 | **the gap ledger** for SOTA, GNN and MLP on all six, by hop slice | laptop | G1, P1 |

Each row is a decision point. A stage that fails is filed and closed; nothing is carried forward unrun.

the order from here. docs/PROGRAM_2026_10_09.md's results stand. Each stage gets its own declared file, committed before

## What "SOTA" means on our corpora (fixed now)

The published systems ran on small corpora (HippoRAG 2 on B1's 6-12k passages), or were given each question's topic
entity (the KB-QA line). On our corpora, a SOTA pool or model is that system's construction, run on our corpus.

**LLM-free reproductions, run here on our encoder and our graph:**
- **HippoRAG 2's walk.** A personalized PageRank restarted from every retrieved row at its retrieval weight (step 4e's
  A1 restart), and the same with HippoRAG's node specificity (A2). Its pool is the walk's top N, and its ranking is the
  walk's mass.
- **The KB-QA line (ReaRev, GNN-RAG, NuTrea).** PageRank-Nibble around the question's topic entities, top m. ReaRev's
  preprocessing uses m = 2,000 on WebQSP and 500 on MetaQA. It is run twice:
  - from the **assigned** topic entities, which is their exposure (a diagnostic column, never our input);
  - from **our linked** entities, by PullNet's exact match (step 4e's A3 linking).
- **Dense, SPLADE and RRF top N** on our encoder (our own first stage).

**The published systems as released** (their encoder and their LLM-built graph) are the user's decision (see the last
section). Until then, their published numbers stay calibration lines, with their exposure named.

**Beating a pool.** At every pool size up to ours, our coverage is at or above the SOTA pool's, on both measures:
- every gold in the pool (ALL);
- gold recall.

On the KB, it also means reaching the assigned-entity pool's coverage without the assigned entity.

## P5: a fourth offset attempt, and what changes

The three filed attempts are the starting point. Each change below answers one of their failures.
- **Capacity.** Each node gets its own learned vector, fit to its corpus's graph: a skip-gram or translation objective
  over the graph's edges, as word2vec and TransE do. The earlier attempts mapped the frozen vectors through one shared
  64-wide matrix. The frozen vector is never changed: it is the learned vector's start and its prior.
- **EM.**
  - The E-step assigns each edge its latent relation (passage graphs have none written down), and each training
    question its latent chain from seeds to golds.
  - The M-step updates the node vectors, the relation offsets and the map from question to chain.
  - On a new graph, the E-step and the node fit run at index time with no labels. Relation offsets come from relation
    names through the model's own map, so an unseen relation still gets one.
- **Reading without walking.** The targets are seed + r1 (one hop) and seed + r1 + r2 (two hops). Their nearest nodes
  in the corpus come from a vector index:
  - the pool takes them;
  - the model reads their distances as inputs.

  These are fixed before any row is scored, so the MLP stays MP-free. The index-time fit's cost and memory go in the
  cost table's graph-index column.
- **The harm at read is tested first** (zgf and zgr lost through the head's score on the passage graphs). P5a reads the
  space as a retriever on its own before any model reads it. The question it answers: from the s1eval seeds, at an
  equal candidate count, does the space find more of the golds that today's pools miss, and of those zsp ranks below 5,
  than the graph's own hops from the same seeds? Its rule is fixed in its own file. A space that finds fewer goes no
  further.
- **One model.** The node table, the offsets, the chain map and the scorer are one model's parameters. The family's GNN
  is the same model plus message passing.
- **The encoder gap.** The space's nearest neighbours of the question itself are a first-stage channel beside dense and
  SPLADE. They are read on hotpotqa and squad, whose misses are the first stage's (4g).

## The user's rule (13 October, about 10:40): the pools first, with nothing learned

"no no until the pools are finalized there should be no learning the offset etc was for reranking the links and pool
finalization are dataset agnostic and parameter free and should have the most golds in least nodes then we go with
the node space EM etc and learned offsets what we discussed".

**The order:**
1. **Links** (P2, with P2c's LLM-free triples).
2. **Pool finalization:** one rule for all six datasets with nothing learned. It is judged by golds per node: coverage
   at every pool size against the best SOTA pool order, on all six.
3. **Only then** the node-space EM (P5's representation) and the learned offsets, as **rerankers** on the final pools,
   with the models retrained on those pools.

**Parameter-free means:**
- no weight is fit to golds or labels;
- nothing is set per dataset;
- the constants are fixed published ones (a walk's damping, RRF's constant), declared before any number.

**What changes:**
- **MX, RC and MXU are learned.** P1b's mixture, P1b2's rank gate and P2b's family gate are fit by EM to the golds.
  They stay beside as learned upper lines and are never the pool.
- **P1c is held** (f8da4b0). Its 124 unsent host items are commented out until the pools are final; it retrained the
  models on MX's pools.
- **P3b's routing to P5 is reranking work.** It waits for the final pools.
- **The next stage is P1d** ([P1D_FREE_POOL.md](P1D_FREE_POOL.md)). Six fixed rules over HippoRAG 2's restart and our
  corroborated links, walked on the two graphs every dataset has, and the first-stage lists. One rule is chosen on
  s1sel and called against P1a's bar on s1eval.

## Decisions that are the user's (defaults until told otherwise)

1. **Running the published systems as released, on our corpora.**
   - HippoRAG 2 needs an LLM pass over every passage: about 11.2 million on 2wiki and hotpotqa.
   - NV-Embed-v2's vectors for those passages would take about 92 GB (16-bit, 4,096 wide).
   - GFM-RAG's, G-reasoner's and ReaRev's checkpoints and code are small downloads (B2).

   **Default:** none is run. Only the LLM-free reproductions above run. The host cannot download (the lab's
   TLS-inspecting firewall), so any download is made on the laptop and pushed.
2. **Host disk.** 28.7 GB is free.
   - P1a's walks write little.
   - P5's node tables (64-wide float32) take about 1.3-1.5 GB each on hotpotqa and 2wiki.
   - Clean-up 15 (about 143 GB) is the user's command.
3. **Representation granularity.** **Default:** vectors per node and per relation only, nothing finer than a node, so
   the 8 October rule holds. Word-level vectors over passage text would relax that rule, and only the user can relax it.
4. **The speed claim per family.** **Default:** both families keep their definitions. Whether 2-3× is reached is
   decided after P5a and P4, never by dropping a win.
